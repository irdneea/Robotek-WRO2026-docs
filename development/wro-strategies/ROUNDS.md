# Index of the simulator rounds

Generated by [`tools/index_rounds.py`](tools/index_rounds.py) from the two ledgers. Do not edit it by hand.

Every attempt was recorded, failed and badly prepared ones included, so these counts are **not a success rate** of the final code: they mix old and new strategies, several car models, diagnostics and experiments that were meant to find a limit. Compare rounds only when the car model, the field and the code are the same.

"The check" is our own acceptance rule (full estimated points, no fault, no contact, a minimum margin and an observed stop). Points are estimates of a rule judge on simulator data. None is an official score.
